Quick Chess Mini Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Quick Chess Mini respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the app collects, uses, and stores.

Data collection

Quick Chess Mini does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal user data.

Local data

The app may store gameplay-related information locally on your device, such as game progress, selected difficulty, selected visual theme, puzzle progress, statistics, and premium unlock status. This information remains on your device and is not sent to our servers.

Purchases

Quick Chess Mini uses Google Play Billing to process optional one-time premium purchases. Payment information is handled by Google Play. Quick Chess Mini does not receive or store your payment card details.

Children

Quick Chess Mini is not specifically directed at children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party services

The app uses Google Play services for app distribution and billing functionality.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
